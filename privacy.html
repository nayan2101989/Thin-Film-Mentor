Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

In short: the numbers and files you enter stay on your device. The offline app counts anonymous visits without cookies. Questions you ask the online AI mentor are handled by Claude (Anthropic) under Anthropic's own privacy policy, and the builder cannot see them.

Who runs this tool

Thin-Film Lab Mentor is a free educational tool built and run by Mehedi Hasan, EIT, an individual based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, Canada. It is not run by a company or university. Contact: message the builder on LinkedIn.

The offline app (this website)

  • Your inputs stay on your device. Calculator values, uploaded J–V files and library searches are processed in your browser. They are never sent to the builder or anyone else.
  • Anonymous visit statistics. We use Umami, a privacy-focused analytics service, to count visits. It does not use cookies and is designed not to collect personal information. It records things like page views, the website that sent you, your browser and device type, and your approximate country. We use this only to understand how many people use the tool. Umami keeps this data for up to 6 months on our plan.
  • Offline storage. To work without internet, the app saves its own files on your device (a browser cache). You can remove them by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser's site data.
  • Hosting. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The online AI mentor (on Claude)

  • "Ask the AI mentor" opens a page hosted on claude.ai. Using it requires a Claude account, and your questions and any images you attach are processed by Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, under Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • The builder does not receive or see the questions you ask or the answers you get.
  • If you send the builder a problem report or message on LinkedIn, the builder will see what you send there and use it only to fix or improve the tool.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We don't ask for your name, email or account to use the offline app.

Please don't enter confidential data

Don't put confidential, proprietary or export-controlled information (for example, your employer's process recipes) into the online AI mentor. The offline calculators keep everything on your device, but the AI mentor sends your question to Claude.

Who this is for

The tool is meant for university students, researchers and engineers. It is not directed at children.

Your choices and rights

You can use the offline app without giving any personal information, and you can block analytics with a content blocker. Because we don't collect personal information through the offline app, there is usually nothing to access or delete. If you have a privacy question or request, contact the builder on LinkedIn. Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA, applies where relevant.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
